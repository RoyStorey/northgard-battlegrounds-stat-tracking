How the numbers work

Late rounds are harder and rare structures appear late, so raw win rates would make late-game pieces look bad. Each battle gets an expected win chance from your own record in that season. A piece's score is wins above that expectation, pulled toward zero when it has only a few battles. Pieces you build together share credit, so check the pairs tab before reading too much into one structure.
