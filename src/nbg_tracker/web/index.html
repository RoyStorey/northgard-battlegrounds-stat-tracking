    What's working

    How is "effect" worked out?

    Each of your PvP battles is compared with similar battles: the same season, a similar record so far in the run, and a similar amount of board. A piece's effect is the extra wins per 100 battles you got with it on the board, beyond what that situation already explains. All pieces are measured together, so pieces you usually build side by side share the credit, and a piece you only add once a run is already winning doesn't take credit for those wins. Pieces seen in only a few battles are pulled toward zero until there's more evidence.
